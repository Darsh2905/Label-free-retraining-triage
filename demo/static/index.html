  What is real here, and what is not?
  
    
    
  • Computed live, every day (about 0.6 s): the deployed LightGBM model classifies that day's real 2022 flows; the eight signals, the drift score, the change detector, the cost rule and every retrain decision come from the project's own code.
    • 
    
  • Pre-trained offline: when the monitor retrains, it loads the model that the experiments trained on the same two weeks of labels (same procedure), and logs the label read. Exception: after you inject drift, a retrain trains a model live on the perturbed flows with a smaller budget (60 rounds, at most 40k flows, about 12 s).
    • 
    
  • Hidden from the monitor: the true macro-F1 curve is computed by the evaluation harness for display only. The grey dashed "never retrain" line was precomputed offline on the same data.
    • 
    
  • Injected drift: scales the packet and byte counts of all new flows; the monitor sees only the changed features, labels are unchanged.
    •
